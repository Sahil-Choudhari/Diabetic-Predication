Prevention & Management

Take proactive steps to prevent Type 2 diabetes or manage existing diabetes effectively

Prevention is Powerful

Research shows that lifestyle changes can reduce the risk of developing Type 2 diabetes by up to 58%. It's never too late to start making positive changes for your health.

Key Prevention Steps
Maintain Healthy Weight

Losing 5 - 7% of body weight can significantly reduce diabetes risk

Practical Tips :
  • Set realistic weight loss goals
  • Track your progress regularly
  • Focus on sustainable changes
  • Celebrate small victories
Stay Physically Active

Regular exercise helps control weight and improves insulin sensitivity

Practical Tips :
  • Aim for 150 minutes of moderate activity per week
  • Include strength training twice weekly
  • Take regular breaks from sitting
  • Find activties you enjoy
Eat Healthy Foods

A balanced diet supports blood sugar control and overall health

Practical Tips :
  • Choose whole grains over refined
  • Eat plenty of fruits and vegetables
  • Limit sugary drinks and snacks
  • Control portion sizes
Get Quality Sleep

Poor sleep can increase diabetes risk and affect blood sugar

Practical Tips :
  • Aim for 7-9 hours nightly
  • Maintain consistent sleep schedule
  • Create a relaxing bedtime routine
  • Limit screen time before bed
Dietary Guidelines

Choose More

  • Whole grains (brown rice, quinoa, oats)
  • Lean proteins (fish, chicken, beans)
  • Healthy fats (nuts, avocado, olive oil)
  • Non-starchy vegetables
  • Fruits in moderation

Limit

  • Sugary beverages and desserts
  • Refined carbohydrates (white bread, pasta)
  • Processed and red meats
  • Trans fats and saturated fats
  • High-sodium foods

Regular Medical Monitoring

If you have prediabetes or risk factors for diabetes, regular checkups are essential:

Blood Sugar Tests

Get tested annually or as recommended by your doctor

A1C Tests

Monitors average blood sugar over 2-3 months

Blood Pressure

Keep blood pressure below 140/90 mmHg

Cholesterol

Monitor lipid levels regularly

Start Today

Small changes can make a big difference. Talk to your healthcare provider about creating a personalized prevention or management plan that works for you.
