Symptoms of Diabetes

Recognizing the warning signs can lead to early diagnosis and better health outcomes

Increased Thirst

Feeling unusually thirsty and needing to drink more fluids than normal

Frequent Urination

Needing to urinate more often than usual, especially at night

Fatigue

Feeling tired and lacking energy despite adequate rest

Blurred Vision

Vision problems or changes in eyesight

Slow Healing

Cuts and wounds that take longer than normal to heal

Unexplained Weight Loss

Losing weight without trying or changing diet

Type 1 Diabetes
  • Sudden, noticeable symptoms
  • Extreme thirst and hunger
  • Rapid weight loss despite eating
  • Bedwetting in children who were dry
Type 2 Diabetes
  • May have no symptoms for years
  • Gradual onset of symptoms
  • Tingling or numbness in hands/feet
  • Frequent infections
⚠️ Emergency Symptoms (Seek Immediate Medical Attention)

These symptoms may indicate diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA) or hyperosmolar hyperglycemic state (HHS), which are medical emergencies:

⚠️ Fruity-smelling breath
⚠️ Nausea and vomiting
⚠️ Shortness of breath
⚠️ Dry mouth
⚠️ Weakness or fatigue
⚠️ Confusion or difficulty concentrating
⚠️ Abdominal pain
⚠️ Loss of consciousness
When to See a Doctor

If you notice any of these symptoms, especially if they persist or worsen, consult your healthcare provider. Early detection and treatment can prevent serious complications.
